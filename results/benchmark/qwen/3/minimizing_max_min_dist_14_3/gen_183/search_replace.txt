This change enhances the adaptive population sizing in differential evolution by implementing a more sophisticated mechanism that monitors both absolute improvement and relative improvement rates. Instead of just counting stagnation iterations, we now track the actual improvement values and use a more nuanced approach to determining when to increase/decrease population size. This should lead to better exploration-exploitation balance and improved convergence to higher-quality solutions.

The key improvements include:
1. Tracking both absolute and relative improvement thresholds
2. Using a more intelligent population sizing strategy based on recent convergence behavior
3. Adding early stopping criteria to avoid unnecessary computation
4. Improving the logic for when to adjust population size
