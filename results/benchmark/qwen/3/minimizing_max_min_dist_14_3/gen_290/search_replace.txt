<DESCRIPTION>
The current icosahedral initialization approach has several weaknesses:
1. It uses a fixed subdivision depth that doesn't guarantee exactly 14 points
2. The point selection process is not optimal for the specific 14-point problem
3. The perturbation is too uniform and may not provide enough diversity

I'll enhance the initialization by creating a more sophisticated approach that:
1. Uses a known optimal 14-point distribution on sphere from mathematical literature
2. Applies more strategic perturbations that preserve good geometric properties
3. Ensures better coverage and avoids clustering
