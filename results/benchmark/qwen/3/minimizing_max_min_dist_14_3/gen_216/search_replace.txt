This improvement focuses on two key areas to boost performance:
1. Enhanced initialization using a more sophisticated spherical distribution method based on electrostatic repulsion
2. Improved adaptive optimization with better convergence detection and population sizing strategies

The changes include:
- Replacing the simple Fibonacci-based initialization with a more robust electrostatic repulsion method
- Adding better convergence monitoring for differential evolution
- Implementing a more intelligent adaptive population sizing strategy
- Using a combination of different local optimization methods with smarter fallback strategies
- Adding early stopping criteria to prevent unnecessary computations

These modifications should help escape local minima more effectively and achieve better convergence toward the optimal solution.
