The key insight is to dynamically adjust the population size during differential evolution based on convergence behavior. The current implementation uses a fixed population size of 20, which may not be optimal throughout the entire optimization process. In the early stages, a larger population helps with exploration, while later stages benefit from smaller populations for exploitation. We'll implement an adaptive mechanism that monitors the relative improvement in objective function value across generations and increases population size when convergence stalls.

This approach follows successful patterns from previous generations where adaptive parameter tuning improved results, particularly in balancing exploration vs exploitation during optimization.
