<DESCRIPTION>
Implement a multi-scale local search approach that alternates between coarse and fine-grained optimizations. This approach uses larger position perturbations initially to escape local optima, followed by smaller adjustments as optimization progresses. This technique has proven effective in circle packing problems because it balances global exploration with local exploitation, leading to better convergence than uniform small steps.

The key insight is to use different step sizes based on optimization iteration:
- Early iterations (0-200): ±0.1 for broad exploration
- Middle iterations (200-400): ±0.05 for medium refinement
- Later iterations (400-500): ±0.01 for fine-tuning

This approach leverages the fact that early exploration needs larger moves to escape poor local optima, while later stages benefit from precise adjustments.
