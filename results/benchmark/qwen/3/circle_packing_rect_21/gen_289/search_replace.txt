<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement enhances the local search algorithm by incorporating gradient-based optimization techniques. The key insight is to compute gradient information from overlapping circle forces and use it to guide the search direction, which will help escape local optima and converge faster to better solutions. This builds upon the existing local search but adds a more sophisticated movement strategy that considers the physical forces between circles.

The change modifies the `local_refinement_step` function to incorporate gradient computation and gradient-guided moves, replacing some of the purely random search strategies with more informed directional updates.
