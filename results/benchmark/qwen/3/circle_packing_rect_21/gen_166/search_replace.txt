<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement implements an adaptive multi-scale local search that dynamically adjusts search strategies based on convergence behavior. The key insight is to use different search intensities and step sizes depending on whether we're in an exploration phase (early iterations) or exploitation phase (later iterations). This approach uses a gradient-like estimation to determine search aggressiveness and adapts the neighborhood search size accordingly.

The change introduces:
1. Dynamic step size adjustment based on convergence rate
2. Multi-scale search with varying neighborhood sizes
3. Convergence monitoring to switch between aggressive and fine-search modes
4. Better boundary exploitation with adaptive boundary bias
