<DESCRIPTION>
Implement a multi-scale local search strategy that alternates between large and small perturbations to escape local optima and refine solutions. This approach uses different step sizes for position adjustments based on optimization phase, starting with larger moves to explore the solution space and gradually refining with smaller adjustments. This addresses the limitation of the current uniform small perturbations that may get stuck in suboptimal local solutions.
