<DESCRIPTION>
This change introduces an adaptive multi-scale local search that dynamically adjusts search intensities based on convergence patterns. The key insight is that early iterations benefit from broader exploration with larger step sizes, while later iterations require fine-tuning with smaller steps. By monitoring improvement rates, the algorithm can automatically shift between coarser and finer search scales, leading to better exploitation of promising regions while maintaining sufficient exploration.

The implementation modifies the `voronoi_guided_local_search` function to:
1. Track recent improvements to detect convergence patterns
2. Dynamically adjust step sizes based on recent performance
3. Implement a more sophisticated move strategy that balances exploration and exploitation
4. Use adaptive iteration counts for different refinement phases

This addresses the limitation of fixed-step-size approaches that may get stuck in local optima or waste time on inefficient searches.
