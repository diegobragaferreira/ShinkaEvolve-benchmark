<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement introduces a hybrid local search approach that combines the strengths of stochastic local search with gradient-based refinement techniques. The key insight is to use the existing stochastic search to explore the solution space broadly, but then apply a more targeted gradient-based optimization to refine promising configurations. This approach leverages the fact that once we have a reasonably good configuration from the evolutionary process, we can use mathematical optimization to fine-tune positions more precisely.

The improvement focuses on the local refinement phase where we replace simple random move searching with a combination of:
1. More intelligent directional searches that consider gradients of radius constraints
2. Mathematical optimization of circle positions via constrained optimization techniques
3. Better handling of convergence behavior to avoid getting stuck in local optima

This should improve the final solution quality by enabling more precise optimization of the final configuration.
