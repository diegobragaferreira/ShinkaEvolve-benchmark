<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement introduces a multi-objective optimization framework that balances maximizing radius sum with maintaining positional stability and avoiding constraint violations. Instead of purely greedy radius maximization, the algorithm now evaluates candidate moves based on both immediate radius gains and potential penalties from positional instability or overlap violations. This addresses the limitation of the current approach which can get stuck in local optima due to its single-objective nature.
