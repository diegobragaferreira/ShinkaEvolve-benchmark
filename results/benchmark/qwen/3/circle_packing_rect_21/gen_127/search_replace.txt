<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement enhances the local search phase by incorporating gradient-based guidance for circle positioning. Instead of purely random or grid-based perturbations, it uses a more sophisticated approach where each circle's movement is guided by the gradient of the radius-sum objective function. This involves calculating how small movements affect the total radius sum and using this information to make more informed decisions about where to place circles for maximum benefit. This should help escape local optima and find better configurations than the current purely exploratory approach.
