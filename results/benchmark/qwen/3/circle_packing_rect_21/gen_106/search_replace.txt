This approach will place initial circle centers based on Voronoi cells that are then optimized to maximize radii.

The key insight is that Voronoi-based initialization naturally distributes points more evenly across space compared to regular grids, leading to better starting configurations for the optimization process.

        # This approach provides better spatial distribution than simple hexagonal packing

        rows = 4
        cols = 6

        for row in range(rows):

                if row % 2 == 1:
                    x += hex_offset
                y = (row + 1) * row_spacing
                positions.append([x, y])

        # Add extra random points to improve distribution
        random_points_needed = self.n_circles - len(positions)
