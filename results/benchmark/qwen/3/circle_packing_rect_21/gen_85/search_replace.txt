<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to enhance the constraint violation handling by implementing an adaptive penalty system that dynamically adjusts based on the optimization phase and violation severity. This builds upon the existing multi-phase approach but adds intelligence to how violations are detected and handled, particularly in early phases where exploration is crucial versus later phases where strict adherence becomes more important.

The change introduces:
1. An adaptive violation threshold that decreases over iterations
2. A dynamic penalty weighting system based on violation count and phase
3. Improved early termination logic that considers both violation count and solution quality
4. Better handling of near-violations that could lead to future conflicts

This addresses a weakness in the current implementation where the same fixed thresholds are used throughout all phases, which may cause either premature termination during exploration or overly strict enforcement during refinement.
