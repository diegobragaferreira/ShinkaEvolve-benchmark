I'll implement a multi-objective optimization approach that balances maximizing sum of radii with maintaining good positional stability. Instead of just focusing on radius maximization, I'll introduce a penalty system that discourages large positional changes that might destabilize the configuration. This will help avoid getting trapped in local optima while still pursuing radius maximization.

The key insight is to use a weighted objective function that considers both immediate radius gain and potential penalties from overlap violations or excessive positional displacement. This builds on successful optimization strategies that use principled trade-offs rather than purely greedy approaches.
