<DESCRIPTION>
This change implements an adaptive multi-scale local search approach that dynamically adjusts search granularity and step sizes based on convergence behavior. The key insight is to use larger steps during early exploration phases when solutions are likely far from optimal, then reduce step sizes during fine-tuning to avoid overshooting local optima. This builds upon the existing evolutionary approach by enhancing the local refinement phase with intelligent scaling that adapts to the optimization landscape.

The implementation monitors the rate of improvement during local search iterations and adjusts the perturbation magnitudes accordingly:
- When rapid improvement occurs (> 0.01 improvement per iteration), use larger step sizes (0.05)
- When improvement slows (< 0.001 per iteration), use smaller step sizes (0.01)
- For intermediate cases, use moderate step sizes (0.025)

This approach helps escape poor local optima while maintaining precision near optimal solutions, which should improve the overall radius sum compared to the fixed-step approach in the baseline.
