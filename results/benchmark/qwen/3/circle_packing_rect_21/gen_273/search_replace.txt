This improvement enhances the hexagonal packing initialization by incorporating Voronoi diagram analysis to better distribute initial circle positions. The key insight is that Voronoi centroids provide natural partition boundaries that can guide more optimal placement of initial seeds. This approach addresses the limitation of the current program's relatively simple hexagonal grid initialization by using geometric analysis to identify high-value regions for circle placement, leading to better spatial coverage and potentially higher radius sums.

The implementation adds:
1. Voronoi analysis to the initialization phase to identify optimal seed positions
2. Strategic placement of initial circles based on Voronoi cell centroids
3. Improved boundary utilization through Voronoi-derived positioning
4. Better distribution of initial seeds across the rectangle
