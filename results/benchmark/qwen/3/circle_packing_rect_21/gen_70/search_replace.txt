This approach helps avoid getting stuck in poor local optima while maintaining solution validity in later stages.

The key insight is to gradually tighten the overlap constraints during optimization - initially allowing slightly overlapping configurations to explore promising regions, then progressively requiring stricter non-overlap as we approach convergence.
