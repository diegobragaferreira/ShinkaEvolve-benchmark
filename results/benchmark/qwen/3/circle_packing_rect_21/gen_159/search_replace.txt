<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement introduces a more sophisticated multi-scale optimization approach that dynamically adapts the optimization strategy based on the current state of the solution. Instead of fixed phases, it uses a dynamic scaling factor that adjusts the learning rate and perturbation magnitudes based on solution quality and convergence behavior. This approach allows for aggressive exploration in early stages, moderate refinement in middle stages, and careful fine-tuning in later stages, potentially achieving better results than the fixed-phase approach that was previously used.
