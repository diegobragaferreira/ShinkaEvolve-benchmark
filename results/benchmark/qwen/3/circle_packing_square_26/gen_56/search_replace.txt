Voronoi diagrams naturally lead to well-separated points which should help avoid poor local optima early in evolution. The idea is to generate seed points using a Voronoi-like distribution first, then assign appropriate radii based on distances to neighbors, which should create better starting configurations that are closer to optimal solutions from the beginning.
