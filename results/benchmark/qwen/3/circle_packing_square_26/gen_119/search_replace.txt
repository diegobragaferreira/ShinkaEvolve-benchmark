<DESCRIPTION>
Adding adaptive penalty scaling that dynamically adjusts the penalty multipliers based on the optimization progress. This approach will allow for more aggressive exploration in early generations when constraint violations are expected to be high, while enforcing stricter constraints in later generations when solutions are closer to optimal. This mimics successful approaches from previous generations that used temporal constraint management to balance exploration vs exploitation.

The idea is to implement a penalty multiplier that decreases exponentially with generations, allowing for less stringent constraint enforcement early on when the population is diverse and exploring the search space, and then becoming more strict as the algorithm converges toward better solutions.

            gx_min = max(0, int((x - r) / self.grid_size))
            gx_max = min(grid_width, int((x + r) / self.grid_size) + 1)

            for gx in range(gx_min, gx_max):
