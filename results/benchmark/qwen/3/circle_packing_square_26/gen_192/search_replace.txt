<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement introduces two key enhancements to boost performance:
1. Progressive constraint relaxation that reduces penalty weights in early generations to allow more aggressive exploration
2. Fitness sharing mechanism to preserve genetic diversity by penalizing similar individuals in the population

These changes address the common weaknesses of evolutionary algorithms in circle packing - premature convergence due to overly strict early penalties and loss of diversity leading to local optima trapping.
