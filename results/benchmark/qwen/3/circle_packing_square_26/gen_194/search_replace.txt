<DESCRIPTION>
The current local refinement implementation uses a fixed force scaling multiplier that doesn't adapt well to different stages of optimization or varying overlap severities. I'll enhance it by implementing an adaptive force scaling system that adjusts based on:

1. Generation stage (early vs late)
2. Overlap severity (how much they're overlapping)
3. Circle size ratios (to avoid over-moving smaller circles)
4. Convergence state (when approaching optimal solution)

This will provide more intelligent overlap resolution that's both more effective and stable during the optimization process.
