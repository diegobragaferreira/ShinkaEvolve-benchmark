<DESCRIPTION>
This modification implements a nested evolutionary-local optimization loop that alternates between high-level evolutionary search (perturbing positions/orientations) and low-level local optimization (using L-BFGS-B for precise adjustments). This approach combines the broad exploration capabilities of evolutionary algorithms with the precision of local optimization to potentially achieve better packing densities. The key insight is that evolutionary algorithms are good at finding promising regions of the search space, while local optimization can refine solutions to convergence. This hybrid approach should help push the score beyond the current 0.1 benchmark toward the target of 0.2544.
