<DESCRIPTION>
The current local optimization uses a mix of L-BFGS-B, Nelder-Mead, and coordinate descent strategies. However, these approaches could be enhanced with gradient-guided refinement that leverages the smooth landscape of the optimization problem. This approach would incorporate gradient information to accelerate convergence and find tighter packings, especially in later evolutionary generations when solutions are already relatively good. The addition of gradient information would help escape local optima more effectively and improve convergence speed.
