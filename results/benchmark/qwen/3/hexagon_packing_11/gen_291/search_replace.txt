<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement focuses on improving both the initial configuration generation and the local optimization strategy to achieve better packing density. The key improvements are:

1. **Enhanced Initial Configuration**: Instead of using a simple hexagonal lattice pattern, I'm implementing a more sophisticated approach that starts with a known good configuration (based on the benchmark) and applies small perturbations to escape local minima while maintaining good spatial distribution.

2. **Improved Local Optimization**: The local optimization now includes a hybrid strategy that combines multiple optimization techniques with better error handling and validation. It also implements a more aggressive early termination when optimization shows little improvement.

The changes aim to increase the likelihood of finding configurations closer to or exceeding the benchmark of 1/outer_hex_side_length = 1/3.930092 ≈ 0.2544.
