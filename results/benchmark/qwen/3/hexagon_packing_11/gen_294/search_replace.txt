<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement improves the local optimization phase by implementing adaptive perturbation strategies that adjust based on the optimization landscape. Instead of using fixed step sizes, the algorithm dynamically adjusts perturbation magnitudes based on recent improvement trends and incorporates both gradient-based and grid-based search strategies for better exploration-exploitation balance. This approach helps escape local minima more effectively while maintaining convergence speed.
