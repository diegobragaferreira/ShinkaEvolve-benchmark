<DESCRIPTION>
I'll implement an adaptive mutation rate that decreases over generations to balance exploration in early stages with exploitation in later stages. This approach will help avoid premature convergence while allowing the algorithm to fine-tune solutions as it progresses through generations.

The key insight is that in evolutionary algorithms, it's often beneficial to start with higher mutation rates to explore the search space broadly, then reduce mutation rates as the population converges toward good solutions to avoid disrupting promising individuals.
