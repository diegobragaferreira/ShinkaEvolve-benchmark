I'll implement an adaptive mutation rate that decreases over generations to improve convergence. This follows the successful pattern from prior implementations where mutation rates were gradually reduced to balance exploration and exploitation. The idea is to start with higher mutation to explore the search space broadly, then reduce it to refine the best solutions found.

Looking at the current code, I notice that the mutation rate stays fixed at 0.3 throughout all generations. By implementing an adaptive scheme that reduces mutation probability over time, we should achieve better convergence to high C₂ values.
