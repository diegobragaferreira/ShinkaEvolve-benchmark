<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to apply the @njit decorator to the core convolution computation functions to significantly boost performance. Currently, the compute_autoconvolution_norms_fast function has the @njit decorator but the underlying computation loops are still relatively slow due to Python overhead. By applying @njit to the critical inner loops and ensuring all operations are JIT-compatible, we can achieve substantial speedup without changing the algorithmic approach.

This change will allow the optimization to explore more candidate solutions within the time constraint, leading to better C2 values.
