<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement implements two key enhancements inspired by successful optimization strategies:

1. **Adaptive Population Size**: Instead of keeping population size fixed, we dynamically adjust it based on generation progress to maintain diversity in early stages and promote convergence in later stages.

2. **Local Search Enhancement**: After each generation, we apply a local optimization method (differential evolution) to the top 10% of individuals to refine them before proceeding to the next generation. This helps escape local optima and improves convergence towards global optimum.

These changes build upon the existing hybrid initialization and evolutionary framework, incorporating proven techniques from top-performing programs to achieve better C₂ values.
