Adding Kagome and HCP lattice patterns to the initial configuration generation would leverage proven mathematical arrangements that typically achieve better packing densities. These patterns are known to provide superior starting points for hexagon packing problems compared to generic ring arrangements. The Kagome lattice (based on triangular lattice) and HCP (hexagonal close-packed) arrangements have been shown to produce high-quality packings in similar problems. This should improve the baseline quality and help the optimization converge toward better solutions faster.
