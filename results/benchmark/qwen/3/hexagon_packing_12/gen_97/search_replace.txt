<DESCRIPTION>
Adding symmetry-aware optimization to improve the hexagon packing by maintaining geometric symmetries during optimization while still allowing for optimal positioning. The key insight is that good hexagon packings often have high symmetry, so we should exploit this structure in our optimization approach rather than treating all hexagon positions independently. This will help avoid getting stuck in poor local minima while maintaining mathematical rigor.

The approach involves:
1. Identifying symmetric groups among hexagons (central, first ring, second ring)
2. Optimizing these groups together while respecting their symmetric relationships
3. Using constraint-aware optimization that preserves symmetry properties

    # Group 0: Central hexagon

    # Group 2: Second ring (6 hexagons)

        1: list(range(1, 7)),  # First ring
        2: list(range(7, 12))   # Second ring

    # Flatten initial data for optimization
    initial_flat = initial_hex_data.flatten()

        bounds.extend([(-10, 10), (-10, 10), (0, 360)])

        for group_id, group_indices in sym_groups.items():
