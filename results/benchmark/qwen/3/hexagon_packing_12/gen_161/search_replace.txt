Adding a multi-stage optimization approach that progressively refines the hexagon packing configuration. This involves implementing three distinct refinement stages with decreasing mutation strengths and different optimization strategies:
1. Stage 1: Coarse-grained exploration with higher mutation rates
2. Stage 2: Medium-grained refinement with moderate mutation rates
3. Stage 3: Fine-grained polishing with low mutation rates

This approach allows for broader exploration in early stages followed by precise optimization in later stages, which should help achieve better packing density and approach the target ratio of 0.2537 more effectively.
