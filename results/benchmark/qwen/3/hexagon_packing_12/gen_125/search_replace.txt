<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to enhance the symmetry-aware mutation operator to better exploit the inherent symmetries of hexagon packing while maintaining diversity. The current implementation applies uniform mutations across all positions, but we can make it smarter by:

1. Identifying and preserving the natural symmetry groups in hexagonal arrangements
2. Using geometric relationships to guide mutations rather than pure randomness
3. Implementing adaptive mutation rates based on how well the current configuration maintains symmetry

This approach should help discover better packing arrangements that respect the fundamental hexagonal symmetry while still allowing for effective exploration of the solution space.
