The current best program precisely formulates convolution constraints as linear inequalities and solves them using `scipy.optimize.linprog`, ensuring valid sequences are produced. This leads to robust and structured optimization paths that avoid local minima, offering better convergence properties than purely stochastic approaches, which is evident in the current program’s ability to reach a 0.5 score reliably.

- **Adaptive Parameter Tuning and Dynamic Sequence Adjustment**: The current best program introduces dynamic adaptation of key parameters—such as sequence length scaling and adaptive step sizes—which allows fine-tuned behavior based on input characteristics. This is consistent with *Adaptive Convolutional Evolution* (0.5) and *Evolutionary Autocorrelation Optimizer* (0.4), where adaptive mechanisms prevent premature convergence and enhance exploration. The program’s use of `np.sqrt(2 * n)` scaling and `t = 0.01` for sequence updates shows strategic tuning that contributes to its superior performance.
