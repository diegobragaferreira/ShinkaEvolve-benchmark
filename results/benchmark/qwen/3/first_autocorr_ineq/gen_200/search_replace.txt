This edit improves the curvature estimation in the `get_good_direction_to_move_into` function by implementing a more robust second-order differentiation approach using central differences. This helps in making better directional adjustments during optimization, potentially leading to faster convergence and better final solutions. The enhanced estimation uses a smaller epsilon for improved numerical accuracy and includes better error handling.
