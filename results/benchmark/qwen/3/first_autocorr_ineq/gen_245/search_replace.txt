<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation of curvature estimation in `get_good_direction_to_move_into` uses a simplistic finite difference approach that may not provide accurate second-order information. By improving the curvature estimation with a more robust technique that better captures second-order behavior, we can enhance the optimization direction and thus improve the overall performance.

The key insight is to avoid overfitting to noisy finite difference approximations and instead use a stabilized approach that considers the convolution landscape more carefully. We'll use a more reliable second-order approximation that computes the curvature by evaluating the change in convolution peaks across multiple perturbations rather than relying on single-point finite differences.
