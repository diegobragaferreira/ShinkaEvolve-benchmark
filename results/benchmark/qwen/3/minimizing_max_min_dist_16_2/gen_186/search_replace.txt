The key insight is to improve the Voronoi-based guidance in the perturbation strategy. Instead of just using Voronoi cell areas to identify points for perturbation, we should also consider the Voronoi-based force analysis to determine more intelligent movement directions. This involves computing forces between points based on their Voronoi cells and using these forces to guide perturbations more effectively.

The enhancement adds:
1. A more sophisticated Voronoi-based force calculation that considers both proximity and Voronoi geometry
2. Better integration of Voronoi information into the perturbation process
3. Improved boundary handling that respects Voronoi constraints more carefully
4. More efficient Voronoi computation with caching for better performance
