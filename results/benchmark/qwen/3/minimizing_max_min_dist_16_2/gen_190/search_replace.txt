<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses 8 diverse initial configurations but doesn't fully leverage the multi-start optimization effectively. The proposed improvement adds more sophisticated initialization strategies including:
1. Adding a spherical arrangement as an additional initialization strategy
2. Improving the neighborhood selection logic for coordinated perturbations
3. Enhancing the boundary handling to prevent points from getting trapped at boundaries
4. Increasing the number of multi-start attempts from 8 to 12 for better exploration
5. Adding a more robust early stopping mechanism

This addresses the limitations in the current code where the optimization might get stuck in local optima due to insufficient diversity in starting points and inadequate boundary handling.
