This improvement introduces a more sophisticated adaptive neighborhood move strategy. Instead of randomly selecting 2-4 points for neighborhood moves, I'll implement a strategy that considers the local density and geometric relationships. The key insight is that moving points that are clustered together or are part of specific geometric configurations (like forming small triangles) can lead to better optimization. This builds upon the existing neighborhood_move function but makes it smarter about which points to move together.

The change modifies the neighborhood_move function to:
1. Analyze local point configurations
2. Select more strategically relevant point subsets for coordinated moves
3. Improve the exploration of the solution space
