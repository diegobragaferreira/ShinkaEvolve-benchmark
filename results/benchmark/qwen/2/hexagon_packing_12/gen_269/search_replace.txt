<DESCRIPTION>
The current penalty system uses fixed weights for containment (1e6) and overlap (1e5) violations. This approach doesn't distinguish between the severity of different constraint violations. I'll implement adaptive penalty weighting that assigns higher penalties to more critical constraint violations, particularly containment violations which are fundamental to the problem. This should help the optimizer focus more on feasible solutions and avoid getting stuck in regions with many overlap violations but few containment violations.
