<DESCRIPTION>
The main bottleneck in the current implementation is the repeated geometric calculations in the constraint checking functions. By applying @jit decorators to the core geometric operations like hexagon vertex generation, containment checking, and overlap detection, we can significantly speed up these frequently called functions. This will allow for more iterations within the time constraint and better exploration of the solution space.

The key insight is that the hexagon operations (vertex generation, bounds calculation, spatial hashing) are called very frequently during constraint validation, making them prime candidates for JIT compilation with numba. This change will dramatically reduce computation time for each fitness evaluation while maintaining numerical accuracy.
</DESCRIPTION>
