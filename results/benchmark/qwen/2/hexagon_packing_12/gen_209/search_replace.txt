<DESCRIPTION>
Implement adaptive penalty weighting to better balance containment vs overlap penalties. The current implementation uses a fixed penalty of 1000000 for overlaps, but we can make this adaptive based on constraint violation severity. Containment violations are more fundamental - they mean hexagons are outside the outer hexagon, which is a hard constraint, while overlaps are softer constraints that can be managed with appropriate penalties. By making the penalty for containment violations even higher (1500000) and overlap penalties more nuanced (1000000), we can guide optimization more effectively towards feasible solutions that are also tightly packed.
