Implement adaptive penalty weighting that dynamically adjusts penalty weights based on constraint violation severity. This addresses the issue that containment violations are more fundamental than overlap violations and should be penalized more heavily. The adaptive approach will assign higher penalties (1500 for containment vs 1000 for overlap) to make the optimization landscape smoother while still strongly discouraging constraint violations.

This change will enhance constraint enforcement by making the penalty system more sophisticated and reflective of the true importance of different constraint types in the problem formulation.
