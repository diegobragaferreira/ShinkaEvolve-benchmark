The current implementation uses a combination of fast containment checks and Shapely-based overlap detection, but the overlap detection is inefficient for the 12-hexagon case. I'll implement a more efficient spatial hashing approach for overlap detection, similar to what was suggested in prior versions but integrated more systematically. This will dramatically improve the performance of overlap checking during optimization, allowing for more iterations and better exploration of the solution space within the 180-second time limit.
