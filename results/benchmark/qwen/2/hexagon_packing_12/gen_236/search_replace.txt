<DESCRIPTION>
I'll implement a new approach that incorporates symmetry-aware optimization operators to better exploit the inherent symmetries in the hexagon packing problem. This builds upon the existing symmetry handling but adds more sophisticated symmetry-aware mutations and crossover operations that respect the 6-fold rotational symmetry and reflectional symmetries of the hexagonal arrangement.

The key insight is that the 12-unit hexagons can be grouped into symmetric classes that should maintain their relationships during evolution. For example, opposite hexagons should maintain opposite positions, and diagonal pairs should maintain their diagonal relationships. This will reduce the effective search space and allow for more focused optimization.
