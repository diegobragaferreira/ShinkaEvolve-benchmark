This approach assigns higher penalties to containment violations (since they're more fundamental) and uses dynamic scaling to better guide the optimization towards valid solutions while still allowing for effective exploration.

The key insight is that containment violations are fundamentally more serious than overlap violations because they mean the solution is invalid even before considering overlaps. By making the penalty weights adaptive and more severe for containment issues, we can improve convergence to valid solutions.

    containment_violations = 0
    overlap_violations = 0

    if use_shapely:

            center_x, center_y, angle = inner_hex_data[i]
