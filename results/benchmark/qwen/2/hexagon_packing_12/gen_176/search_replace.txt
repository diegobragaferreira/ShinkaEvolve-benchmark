This change implements adaptive penalty weighting to improve optimization performance. The core idea is to assign different penalty weights based on constraint violation severity - containment violations are penalized more heavily than overlap violations since they represent fundamental constraint violations that make solutions invalid. This approach helps guide the optimizer more effectively towards feasible regions of the solution space.
