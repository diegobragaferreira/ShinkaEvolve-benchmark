<DESCRIPTION>
This change implements adaptive penalty weighting that dynamically adjusts penalty weights based on constraint violation severity. The key insight is to assign higher penalties for containment violations than overlap violations since containment is more fundamental to the problem. This approach improves the optimization landscape by making constraint violations more severe and noticeable, leading to better convergence toward valid solutions while avoiding premature convergence to invalid configurations.
