<DESCRIPTION>
Adding adaptive mutation rate in differential evolution can significantly improve convergence by adjusting the mutation strength based on the optimization progress. This helps balance exploration in early stages and exploitation in later stages, which is crucial for complex hexagon packing problems where we need to escape local optima while refining solutions.

The change modifies the DE parameters to use adaptive mutation rates that decrease over generations, allowing for broader exploration initially and more focused exploitation as the optimization progresses. This approach has proven effective in similar geometric optimization problems and should help us beat the current benchmark.
