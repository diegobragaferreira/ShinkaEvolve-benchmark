This can lead to invalid solutions being accepted during optimization.

I'll enhance the collision detection by:
1. Adding a small epsilon tolerance to the SAT algorithm to handle floating-point precision
2. Implementing more aggressive early rejection using bounding box checks with tighter margins
3. Using more stable distance calculations to avoid numerical artifacts
4. Adding buffer zones for better constraint satisfaction

This should help avoid invalid solutions and make the optimization more robust.
