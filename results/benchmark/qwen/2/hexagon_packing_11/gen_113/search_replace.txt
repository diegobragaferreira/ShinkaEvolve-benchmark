<DESCRIPTION>
Adding parallel differential evolution runs to explore the search space more effectively. This approach runs multiple DE instances with different random seeds and initialization strategies simultaneously, then picks the best solution among all runs. This increases the likelihood of finding better local optima and avoids getting stuck in poor regions of the search space, which is particularly important for complex optimization landscapes like hexagon packing.
