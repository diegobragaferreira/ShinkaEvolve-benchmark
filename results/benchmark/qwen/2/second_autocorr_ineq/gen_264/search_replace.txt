<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement focuses on two key improvements to boost the C₂ constant:
1. Improving peak spacing with more sophisticated logarithmic distribution that avoids clustering
2. Implementing adaptive optimization that selectively targets the most impactful peak parameters
The changes aim to create better autoconvolution profiles with flatter g-functions that maximize the C₂ ratio.

The key insight is that current logarithmic spacing can still produce clusters, and selective optimization of only the most significant peaks will be more efficient and effective than optimizing all parameters.
