The idea is to implement logarithmic spacing for Gaussian peak positions during initialization to improve distribution across multiple scales. This follows the successful pattern seen in previous programs where logarithmic spacing helped cover different frequency ranges better, leading to improved autoconvolution profiles and higher C2 scores.

The change modifies the peak position generation logic in `create_structured_gaussian_individual` to use logarithmic spacing instead of uniform random positioning, which should enhance the function's ability to achieve higher C2 scores by avoiding clustering of peaks in narrow regions.
