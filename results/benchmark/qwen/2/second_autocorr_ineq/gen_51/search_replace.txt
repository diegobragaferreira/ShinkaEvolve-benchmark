<DESCRIPTION>
The main issue with the current adaptive Gaussian construction is that peak positions are generated randomly without considering optimal spacing. By enforcing minimum distances between peaks and using more strategic placement patterns, we can avoid destructive interference in the autoconvolution that leads to poor C2 scores. This modification ensures better distribution of peaks across the domain and reduces likelihood of creating sharp autoconvolution spikes that hurt the C2 ratio.
