The current implementation uses a fixed evolutionary algorithm approach but lacks mechanisms to escape local optima effectively. Based on the successful patterns observed in prior generations (38, 44, 76, 80, 82, 83, 84, 85), I'll implement probabilistic reinitialization that randomly resets 10% of circles with new valid positions when optimization appears to stall. This technique helps escape persistent local optima and explores better regions of solution space, which is crucial for maximizing the sum of radii beyond the current benchmark of 2.3658321334167627.
