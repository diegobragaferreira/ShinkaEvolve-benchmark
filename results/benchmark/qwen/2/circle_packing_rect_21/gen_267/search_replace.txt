<DESCRIPTION>
The proposed enhancement focuses on improving the local refinement phase by implementing a more sophisticated neighborhood search strategy. The current implementation only tries small radius increases for individual circles, but this can get stuck in local optima. I'm introducing a multi-level local search that combines:

1. Neighborhood-based radius increases using spatial indexing
2. Position perturbations with adaptive step sizes
3. Strategic circle repositioning based on overlap analysis
4. Early termination criteria based on improvement rates

This approach will better navigate the solution space and find higher quality local optima compared to the existing single-direction radius increase strategy.
