The current implementation uses approximate normalization which can lead to points drifting away from the unit sphere. I'll enhance the spherical constraint enforcement by implementing stricter projection that maintains exact unit sphere constraints throughout optimization. This is critical for maximizing the min/max ratio since points that stray from the sphere can create artificial distortions in distance calculations.
