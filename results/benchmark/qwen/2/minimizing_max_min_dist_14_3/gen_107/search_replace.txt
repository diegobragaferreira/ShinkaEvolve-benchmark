<DESCRIPTION>
The proposed enhancement focuses on improving the spherical constraint handling in the simulated annealing process. The current implementation uses basic tangent plane projection which can lead to points drifting away from the sphere surface due to numerical errors. This change introduces a more robust projection method that ensures points remain strictly on the unit sphere while preserving the mathematical correctness of tangent plane movements. This should improve convergence and prevent the optimization from getting stuck in invalid configurations.
</DESCRIPTION>
