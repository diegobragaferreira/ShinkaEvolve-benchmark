<DESCRIPTION>
Adding a checkpointing mechanism with restart capability to improve optimization robustness. This enhancement will save the best solution found so far every 30 seconds and implement a restart mechanism that initializes from the best known configuration when no improvement is observed for extended periods. This helps overcome premature convergence and ensures more thorough exploration of the solution space within the time budget.
