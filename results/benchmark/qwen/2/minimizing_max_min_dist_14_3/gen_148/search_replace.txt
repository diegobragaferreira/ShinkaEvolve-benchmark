<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses a fixed exponential cooling rate which may not be optimal for different phases of optimization. I propose implementing an adaptive cooling schedule that dynamically adjusts based on the optimization progress. This approach monitors convergence behavior and adjusts the cooling rate accordingly - speeding up when progress stalls and slowing down when approaching optimal solutions. This should help overcome plateaus in optimization progress and potentially improve the 0.1 score by enabling more efficient exploration of the solution space.
