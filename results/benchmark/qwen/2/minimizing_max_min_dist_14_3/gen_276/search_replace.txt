<DESCRIPTION>
The current adaptive cooling implementation only considers recent improvement rates but doesn't track the quality of the current solution relative to known benchmarks. To improve performance, I'll implement a more sophisticated cooling schedule that considers both the absolute improvement rate and how close we are to the theoretical optimum. This will allow for more intelligent temperature adjustments that can quickly converge when near good solutions while maintaining exploration when far from optimal.

The key insight is to make cooling more aggressive when we're making rapid progress AND when we're approaching reasonable solutions, but be more conservative when progress is slow OR when we're in a region far from good solutions.
