I'll implement an enhanced adaptive perturbation scheme that dynamically adjusts the perturbation strategy based on the current optimization landscape. The key insight is to identify whether points are too close together (clustering) or too far apart (spreading) and apply different perturbation magnitudes accordingly.

This approach addresses the issue that the current implementation might not be sufficiently adaptive in its perturbation strategy, especially during early stages when points are randomly distributed and later stages when they're converging towards optimal positions.
