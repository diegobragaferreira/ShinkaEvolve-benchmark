<DESCRIPTION>
Adding hill climbing refinement to the optimization process. This improvement enhances the final solution quality by applying a local search procedure that makes small perturbations to the best solution found so far, accepting moves that improve the objective function. This helps escape local optima that standard optimization methods might get stuck in and can provide further refinement of the solution. The hill climbing approach is particularly effective for this type of continuous optimization problem where small adjustments can significantly improve the min/max distance ratio.
</DESCRIPTION>
