<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses fixed perturbation magnitudes in the structured grid initialization. This change introduces adaptive perturbation scaling that adjusts the magnitude of random perturbations based on the current state of the point distribution. The idea is to apply larger perturbations when the configuration is poor (low ratio) to encourage exploration of better regions, and smaller perturbations when the configuration is already good to enable fine-tuning without disrupting progress. This should improve convergence characteristics and potentially yield better final solutions.
