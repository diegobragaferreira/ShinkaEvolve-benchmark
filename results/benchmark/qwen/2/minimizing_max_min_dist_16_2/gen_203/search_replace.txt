<DESCRIPTION>
Adding an evolutionary algorithm restart strategy with local refinement to enhance multi-start optimization. This approach combines differential evolution for global exploration with local SLSQP optimization for precise refinement, creating a more robust optimization process that can escape local optima and find better solutions than the current purely local optimization approach.

The key insight is to use evolutionary algorithms like differential evolution as a global search method to find diverse good starting points, then refine these with local optimization. This addresses the limitation of the current approach which relies heavily on manual strategy selection and single-point optimization.
