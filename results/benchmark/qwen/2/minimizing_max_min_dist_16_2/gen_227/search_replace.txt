<DESCRIPTION>
Adding symmetry-breaking constraints to avoid degenerate solutions that may trap the optimization process. This approach fixes positions of certain points (like corner points) to break symmetric equivalencies, ensuring each optimization path explores distinct solution spaces rather than cycling between identical structures. This builds upon the current program's emphasis on robust initialization and constraint handling to help escape problematic local minima.

The implementation adds fixed corner constraints to the optimization process, specifically fixing the positions of the bottom-left, bottom-right, top-left, and top-right corner points to break rotational and reflective symmetries. This prevents the optimizer from getting stuck in symmetrically equivalent but suboptimal configurations.
