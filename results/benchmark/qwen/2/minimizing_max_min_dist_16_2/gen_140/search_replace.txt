<DESCRIPTION>
I'll implement adaptive perturbation scaling in the grid initialization based on distance distribution. This enhancement will dynamically adjust perturbation magnitudes according to the current distribution of distances during early optimization iterations. The idea is to analyze the ratio of minimum to maximum distances at the start of each optimization run, and scale the random perturbation standard deviation inversely with this ratio - smaller perturbations when distances are already well-balanced and larger perturbations when the configuration is highly skewed. This builds on the current program's successful use of structured initialization and small random perturbations, but introduces intelligence to the perturbation process to improve convergence speed and solution quality.
