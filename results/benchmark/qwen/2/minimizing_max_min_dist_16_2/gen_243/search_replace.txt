The current implementation uses various initialization strategies and optimization methods, but lacks explicit symmetry breaking constraints that could prevent the optimizer from converging to degenerate or symmetric solutions. By adding constraints that fix certain points to specific positions (like corners) and imposing lexicographic ordering on remaining points, we can break the inherent symmetries of the problem and guide the optimization toward more unique and potentially better solutions. This approach leverages the fact that many local optima in point dispersion problems are symmetric and can be eliminated through careful constraint design.

The changes involve:
1. Adding a symmetry-breaking constraint that fixes corner points to exact positions
2. Adding a lexicographic ordering constraint to ensure unique relative arrangements
3. Modifying the optimization to respect these constraints properly
4. Improving the initialization to start with asymmetric configurations
