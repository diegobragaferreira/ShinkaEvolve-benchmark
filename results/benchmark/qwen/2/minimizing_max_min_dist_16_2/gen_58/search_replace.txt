The current implementation uses a fixed set of initial configurations and basic multi-stage optimization. This change introduces an adaptive multi-start strategy that dynamically selects the most promising starting points based on gradient information and incorporates gradient-based guidance to accelerate convergence. The key insight is to use the gradient information from initial optimizations to guide the selection of better starting points for subsequent optimizations, rather than relying purely on random perturbations.

This approach leverages the fact that gradient information can reveal which regions of the configuration space are more likely to yield better solutions, allowing us to focus computational resources more effectively.
