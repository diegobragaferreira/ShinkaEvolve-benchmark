<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement adds a spherical Voronoi-based initialization strategy that creates a more uniform distribution of points by leveraging geometric principles. The spherical Voronoi approach naturally produces well-spaced point sets that are particularly effective for maximizing the minimum distance to maximum distance ratio. This is inspired by the mathematical concept that optimal point distributions on spheres tend to be more uniform and evenly spaced, which translates well to planar arrangements when properly projected.

The key insight is that spherical Voronoi diagrams provide a principled way to distribute points uniformly, and this can be adapted to 2D by considering points on a sphere and mapping them to the plane. This approach can significantly improve the initial configuration quality compared to simple grid or random approaches, leading to better convergence in the subsequent optimization steps.
</DESCRIPTION>
