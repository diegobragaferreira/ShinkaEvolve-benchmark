This enhancement introduces a symmetry-breaking mechanism to the multi-start optimization approach. The key insight is that without symmetry breaking, many optimization paths may converge to equivalent but degenerate solutions that differ only by rotation/reflection. By adding explicit symmetry-breaking constraints and using a more sophisticated initialization strategy that naturally avoids symmetrically equivalent arrangements, we can escape these local optima traps and achieve better solutions. Specifically, I'll implement:
1. A new initialization strategy that places some points at fixed positions (breaking rotational symmetry)
2. A symmetry-breaking constraint enforcement during optimization
3. Enhanced diversity in the multi-start approach through more varied initial configurations
