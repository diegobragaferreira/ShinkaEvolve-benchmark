<DESCRIPTION>
The main improvement is to enhance the symmetry-breaking constraints by adding more sophisticated constraints that prevent the optimizer from converging to degenerate or symmetric solutions. The previous version only fixed four corner points, but this can be expanded to include additional constraints like fixing relative positions or enforcing specific ordering patterns. This should help avoid local optima that are symmetrically equivalent but suboptimal.

The key insight is that many optimization algorithms get trapped in symmetric solutions where points can be permuted without changing the objective value. By adding more specific constraints that break these symmetries, we guide the optimization towards genuinely better solutions.
