<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces elite preservation in the evolutionary search to maintain high-performing sequences during evolution. It keeps the top 10% of sequences from each generation to prevent loss of good solutions due to random perturbations and to maintain diversity in the population. This enhancement helps in stabilizing improvement pathways and accelerating convergence toward optimal solutions.
