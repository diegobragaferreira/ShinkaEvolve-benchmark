<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces two key enhancements to improve the optimization process:
1. An adaptive gradient step size that becomes more conservative as the algorithm progresses
2. Elite preservation mechanism that maintains high-performing sequences to prevent loss of good solutions
These changes aim to balance exploration and exploitation, leading to better convergence towards optimal solutions.
