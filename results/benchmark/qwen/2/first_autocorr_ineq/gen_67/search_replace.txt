The main idea is to enhance the convolution LP solving by utilizing FFT-based convolution for constructing the constraint matrix. This improves scalability and numerical stability, especially for larger sequences, since FFT-based convolution is O(n log n) compared to O(n²) for direct convolution. This is expected to make the optimization smoother and more effective, leading to better convergence and higher quality solutions.

The specific change involves modifying `solve_convolution_lp` to construct the constraint matrix using FFT-based convolution techniques, rather than the standard matrix construction. This will improve performance and enable handling of larger sequences more effectively.
