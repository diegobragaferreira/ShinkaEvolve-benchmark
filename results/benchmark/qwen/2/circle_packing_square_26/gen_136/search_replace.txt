<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to implement adaptive grid refinement that dynamically adjusts the grid resolution based on the population's fitness landscape. This approach will help the algorithm escape local optima and explore areas with potentially better solutions. The strategy involves periodically analyzing population diversity and adjusting grid parameters to either increase exploration (coarse grid) or exploitation (fine grid).

This change adds a dynamic grid refinement mechanism that:
1. Monitors population diversity every few generations
2. Adjusts the initial grid density based on fitness variance
3. Uses finer grids when populations show low diversity (exploitation mode)
4. Uses coarser grids when populations show high diversity (exploration mode)
5. This helps balance between exploration and exploitation throughout evolution
