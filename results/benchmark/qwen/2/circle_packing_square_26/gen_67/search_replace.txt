<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to implement an adaptive local search mechanism that dynamically adjusts its search strategy based on the current state of the optimization. This approach combines three distinct search strategies:
1. Global exploration through large radius increases
2. Fine-grained local optimization through small radius adjustments
3. Strategic movement of circles to resolve conflicts

The adaptive mechanism will switch between these strategies based on:
- Current improvement rate (if no progress, switch to global search)
- Overlap violation severity (if severe violations, use strategic movement)
- Population diversity (if low diversity, use more aggressive local search)

This approach should significantly outperform the current simple refinement by making smarter, context-aware adjustments.
