<DESCRIPTION>
I'll implement a three-phase adaptive mutation rate scheduling to improve the exploration-exploitation balance during evolution. This approach will use different mutation rates during different phases of the optimization process: high exploration in early generations, balanced refinement in middle generations, and fine-tuning in late generations. This builds upon the successful adaptive mutation strategies from previous generations and should help achieve better convergence toward the benchmark.

The key insight is that maintaining a consistent decay schedule may not be optimal - instead we want:
1. Phase 1 (generations 1-60): High mutation rate (0.15) for broad exploration
2. Phase 2 (generations 61-120): Moderate mutation rate (0.05) for balanced refinement
3. Phase 3 (generations 121-150): Low mutation rate (0.015) for fine-tuning

This approach should allow for better discovery of promising regions early on, followed by focused optimization.
