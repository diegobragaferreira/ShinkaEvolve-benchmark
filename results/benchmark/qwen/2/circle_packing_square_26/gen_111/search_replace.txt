This approach will help fine-tune the solutions found by the evolutionary algorithm to push the fitness value closer to the benchmark. The local search will use a greedy approach to slightly adjust circle positions and radii to reduce overlaps and increase total radius sum.

This enhancement builds upon the proven effectiveness of local optimization strategies seen in the prior generations, specifically leveraging the fact that evolutionary algorithms often find good regions of the search space but benefit from local refinement to reach peak solutions.
