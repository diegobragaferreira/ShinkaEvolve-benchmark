<DESCRIPTION>
The current local refinement strategy uses a combination of L-BFGS-B optimization and gradient-free methods, but it could benefit from a more sophisticated approach that incorporates physics-based repulsion forces and adaptive refinement strategies. The proposed improvement introduces a multi-stage local refinement process that:

1. First performs a coarse-grained optimization using a faster method like Nelder-Mead
2. Then applies a more detailed optimization with boundary-aware constraints
3. Finally incorporates physics-inspired particle system to resolve remaining overlaps

This approach should better exploit the optimization landscape and push beyond the current 2.2 score toward the benchmark of ~2.636.
