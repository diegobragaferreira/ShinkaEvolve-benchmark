<DESCRIPTION>
Adding local search refinement to the evolutionary algorithm to improve solution quality. This enhancement will apply a local optimization procedure to the best individual in each generation, helping to fine-tune positions and radii to reduce overlaps and increase total radius sum. This approach leverages the strengths of both global evolutionary search and local gradient-based optimization.

The key insight is that while evolutionary algorithms are great for exploring the search space, they often benefit from local refinement to squeeze out additional improvements from promising regions. The local search will attempt small adjustments to each circle's position and radius to improve fitness while maintaining constraints.
