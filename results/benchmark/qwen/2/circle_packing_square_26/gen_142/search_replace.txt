<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses a fixed mutation rate throughout evolution. I'll implement an adaptive mutation rate that decreases gradually over generations to allow for broader exploration in early stages and focused exploitation in later stages. This approach typically leads to better convergence properties and can help escape local optima more effectively than static mutation rates.
