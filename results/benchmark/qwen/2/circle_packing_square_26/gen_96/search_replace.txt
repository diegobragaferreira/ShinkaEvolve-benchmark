The current Voronoi-based initialization approach has room for improvement. The main issues are:
1. The Voronoi implementation is too simplistic and doesn't properly leverage the geometric properties
2. The circle radius estimation is crude and doesn't account for optimal packing density
3. The initialization lacks proper constraint satisfaction checking

I'll enhance the Voronoi initialization by:
1. Improving the Voronoi cell calculation with better boundary handling
2. Implementing a more sophisticated radius estimation based on Voronoi cell areas
3. Adding proper constraint validation to ensure all initial circles are valid
4. Incorporating a refinement step that adjusts positions to better satisfy constraints

This should yield better starting configurations that are more likely to lead to higher sum_radii values.
