<DESCRIPTION>
The current local optimization approach uses iterative methods but could benefit from a more sophisticated gradient-based refinement step. This enhancement adds a local gradient descent refinement that specifically targets the objective of maximizing radii sum while respecting constraints. The approach will work in conjunction with existing local improvement by applying fine-grained adjustments to circle positions to increase radii without violating constraints.

This improvement is inspired by the observation that the existing local_improvement method, while effective, could be made more precise by incorporating gradient information. By using a simple gradient ascent approach on the objective function (sum of radii), we can achieve better results than purely iterative adjustments.

            max_r = min(x, 1-x, y, 1-y)
            if max_r > r:
                # Simple gradient ascent on radius (we want to maximize sum of radii)
                # For each circle, we can increase its radius up to max_r
                # But we also need to consider constraints with neighbors
