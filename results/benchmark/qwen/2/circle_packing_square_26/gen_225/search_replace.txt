<DESCRIPTION>
The current mutation strategy treats all circles equally, but we can improve performance by making mutation more aware of constraint violations. The idea is to implement constraint-aware mutation where the probability and magnitude of mutations are adjusted based on how close each circle is to violating constraints. Circles that are near boundaries or close to overlaps should receive more careful mutation treatment to avoid creating invalid configurations.
