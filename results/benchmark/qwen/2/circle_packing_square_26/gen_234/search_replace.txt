The current local optimization approach in the `_intensive_refinement` method could be improved by implementing a more sophisticated physics-inspired approach. Instead of just moving circles away from conflicts, we could implement a force-based relaxation method that simulates physical repulsion forces between overlapping circles and attraction forces towards optimal positions. This would allow for more natural and effective resolution of complex overlap scenarios that the current approach might struggle with.

The key insight is to model the circles as physical particles with forces acting on them, which should lead to better convergence to valid configurations with larger radii.
