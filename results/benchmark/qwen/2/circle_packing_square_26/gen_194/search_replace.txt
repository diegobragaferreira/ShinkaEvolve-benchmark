I'll implement a more sophisticated crossover operator that uses overlap probability weighting to reduce the likelihood of generating invalid offspring. This addresses the issue from the prior programs where basic uniform crossover could produce many invalid configurations that required expensive repair processes.

The key insight is to weight crossover probabilities based on the overlap risk between corresponding circles in parent solutions. Circles that are close together (high overlap probability) should have higher crossover probability to encourage recombination, while well-separated circles (low overlap probability) should have lower crossover probability to preserve good individual configurations.

This change builds upon the successful constraint-aware crossover patterns from previous generations while being more intelligent about when to combine genes.
