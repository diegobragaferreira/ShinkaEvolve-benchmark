This improvement introduces an adaptive local search strategy that dynamically adjusts the intensity of local optimization based on the quality of the current solution and population diversity. The key insight is that when we have a good solution (high fitness), we should apply more intensive local refinement to squeeze out additional improvements. When solutions are poor, we should focus on global exploration rather than getting stuck in local optima. This approach combines the best aspects of both global search (evolutionary) and local search (gradient-based) strategies.

The change modifies the local refinement phase to be more intelligent about when and how intensely to optimize, which should help us exceed the benchmark of 2.6358627564136983.

                max_radius = min(orig_x, 1-orig_x, orig_y, 1-orig_y)
