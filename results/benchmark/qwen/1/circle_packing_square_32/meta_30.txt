Modify the candidate selection process to prioritize Voronoi vertices that are closer to square boundaries, while still maintaining the systematic point distribution approach that ensures good coverage, thereby increasing the likelihood of finding configurations with larger radii early in evolution.

2. **Introduce fitness-aware adaptive mutation rates**: Build upon the current program's selective mutation strategy by implementing variable mutation strengths that adapt based on individual fitness levels and evolutionary progress, as demonstrated in Generation 19's success with gradual rate reduction. Lower mutation rates for high-fitness individuals while applying higher rates to lower-performing candidates during early generations, using a sigmoidal decay function that reduces mutation intensity as fitness improves, ensuring stable convergence without losing exploration ability.

3. **Add hierarchical penalty function with violation severity scaling**: Enhance the current binary penalty system by introducing a tiered constraint enforcement approach that differentiates between boundary violations and overlap issues, mirroring the successful techniques from Generation 9 and 19 that used properly weighted penalties. Assign quadratic penalty multipliers for boundary constraint violations (since they make solutions completely invalid) versus linear penalties for minor overlaps (which may be acceptable in some contexts), enabling more nuanced guidance during evolution while preventing premature elimination of near-valid solutions.

4. **Incorporate spatial grid indexing for collision detection optimization**: While the current program uses direct geometric validation, integrate a lightweight spatial grid system to dramatically reduce redundant overlap checks, following the performance improvement pattern seen in high-performing programs that avoided complex KDTree failures. Partition the unit square into a 10×10 grid and only check overlaps between circles in adjacent cells, reducing collision detection complexity from O(n²) to approximately O(n) for typical configurations, while maintaining the reliable geometric validation that prevents false positives.

5. **Implement fitness sharing to preserve population diversity**: Strengthen the current tournament selection and elitism framework by adding explicit diversity-preserving mechanisms such as fitness sharing or niche counting, which complement the stable evolutionary approach that has enabled consistent 2.4 scores in previous generations. Calculate niche distances using a combination of positional and radius differences between individuals, then adjust fitness scores to favor less crowded regions of the search space, preventing premature convergence while maintaining the selection pressure that drives improvement towards optimal solutions.
