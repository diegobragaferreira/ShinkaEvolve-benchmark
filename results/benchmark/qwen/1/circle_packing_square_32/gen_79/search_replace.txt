<DESCRIPTION>
The current Voronoi initialization doesn't effectively exploit the strategic placement of circles near square corners and edges where larger radii can be achieved. I'll enhance the Voronoi point generation to explicitly favor corner and edge regions with higher probability weights, which should lead to better initial configurations that can achieve higher sum_radii values.

The key insight is that corners and edges of the unit square provide natural locations for large-radius circles, but the current implementation only adds a few corner points randomly without biasing them appropriately. By making the sampling more systematic and weighted toward strategic locations, we should see improved performance.
