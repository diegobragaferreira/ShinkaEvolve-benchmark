<DESCRIPTION>
The key insight is to enhance the Voronoi initialization by incorporating boundary-weighted Voronoi vertex selection that favors vertices near square boundaries and corners. This addresses the limitation of the current approach which doesn't systematically prioritize locations where larger circles can potentially be placed. By implementing a weighted selection mechanism that gives higher probability to vertices near edges/corners (within 0.1 units), we can systematically improve the initial configuration quality which will lead to better convergence in the evolutionary process.
