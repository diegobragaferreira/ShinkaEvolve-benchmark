<DESCRIPTION>
The current Voronoi initialization uses a fixed-weighting scheme that doesn't adequately prioritize the most promising candidate positions for large circle placements. I'll enhance the Voronoi-based initialization by implementing a more sophisticated candidate selection mechanism that identifies positions with the highest potential for hosting large circles, particularly near boundaries and corners where larger radii are naturally possible.

The key insight is that candidates near the boundary and corners should be weighted much higher since they offer the possibility of larger radii. I'll modify the candidate selection process to compute a more accurate "potential radius" metric for each Voronoi vertex and centroid, then use this to guide the selection of the best starting positions.

        # Weight candidates based on potential for hosting large circles
        def potential_radius_weight(x, y):
            # Maximum possible radius at this position without boundary constraints
            max_radius_free = min(x, 1-x, y, 1-y)

            # Bonus for being near corners (where very large radii can be achieved)
            corner_dist = min(
                np.sqrt((x-0)**2 + (y-0)**2),
                np.sqrt((x-1)**2 + (y-0)**2),
                np.sqrt((x-0)**2 + (y-1)**2),
                np.sqrt((x-1)**2 + (y-1)**2)
