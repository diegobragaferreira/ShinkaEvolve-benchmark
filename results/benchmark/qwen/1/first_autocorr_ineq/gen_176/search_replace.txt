<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements progressive population initialization that evolves based on learned structural patterns from previous generations. The idea is to gradually shift from purely random initialization to incorporating structural traits identified in top-performing sequences from earlier generations. This approach builds upon strategies from "Adaptive Evolutionary Optimization" and "Fast Autocorrelation Evolutionary Search" which have shown to accelerate convergence and improve solution quality.

The key improvements include:
1. Adding a memory mechanism to record dominant features from top sequences
2. Implementing progressive initialization that uses learned patterns
3. Enhancing structural diversity through mixed initialization strategies
4. Introducing a method to adaptively blend different initialization approaches

This should help discover more promising regions of the solution space faster and lead to better overall performance.
