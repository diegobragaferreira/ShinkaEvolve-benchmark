This modification introduces an adaptive Voronoi-based optimization approach that enhances the local search mechanism. Instead of uniform perturbations, we now use Voronoi cell area analysis to determine the importance of each circle in the packing. Circles in high-density regions (smaller Voronoi cells) receive smaller perturbations, while those in low-density regions can be adjusted more aggressively. This leads to more efficient optimization by focusing computational resources where they matter most, improving both convergence speed and final solution quality.

            # Try small perturbations with adaptive step sizes based on Voronoi analysis
            trial = current.copy()

            # Compute Voronoi diagram to understand local density
            voronoi_points = np.array([[c[0], c[1]] for c in trial])
            try:
                vor = Voronoi(voronoi_points)
                # Calculate Voronoi cell areas
                cell_areas = []
                for i in range(len(vor.points)):
                    indices = np.where(vor.point_region == i)[0]
                    if len(indices) > 0:
