<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement introduces a progressive constraint relaxation mechanism during evolution. The idea is to start with relaxed constraints (allowing up to 5% constraint violations) to enable faster exploration of promising regions, then gradually tighten constraints to enforce strict feasibility. This addresses the challenge that overly strict constraints can trap optimization in local minima while still maintaining solution quality. The approach mirrors successful strategies from earlier generations that balanced exploration vs exploitation effectively.

The key insight is that early-stage exploration benefits from being able to temporarily violate constraints to escape poor local optima, while later stages require strict enforcement of all constraints. This creates a more robust optimization trajectory that can better navigate the complex landscape of circle packing problems.

        """Perform enhanced local optimization."""
        current_circles = circles.copy()
        best_circles = current_circles.copy()
        best_fitness = self.problem.evaluate_fitness(current_circles)

        patience_counter = 0
        last_improvement = 0

        for iteration in range(max_iter):

            for i in range(len(current_circles)):

                mutated_pos = self.mutation_engine.mutate_position(current_circles, i, density_scores)
