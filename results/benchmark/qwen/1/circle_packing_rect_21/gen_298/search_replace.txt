<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses Voronoi diagrams for criticality assessment but doesn't fully leverage the spatial information for adaptive mutation. I'll enhance the mutation operator to use Voronoi-based adaptive step sizes more effectively, incorporating the actual Voronoi cell areas to determine optimal mutation magnitudes for both position and radius changes. This approach will allow the algorithm to naturally adapt mutation strength based on local spatial constraints, leading to more efficient exploration of the search space.

The key insight is that Voronoi cell areas directly correlate with local constraint density - smaller cells indicate more constrained regions where smaller mutations are needed to maintain feasibility, while larger cells suggest looser regions where larger mutations can be beneficial.
