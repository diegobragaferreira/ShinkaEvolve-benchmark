<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement introduces Voronoi-based mutation scaling to make the optimization more adaptive to local constraint density. The key insight is that in dense regions where circles are tightly packed, smaller mutations are needed to avoid constraint violations, while in sparse regions larger mutations can be safely applied. This builds on the existing Voronoi density computation but makes it more sophisticated by using the inverse of Voronoi cell areas to determine appropriate mutation step sizes.

The change modifies the `mutate_radius` and `mutate_position` methods to scale mutation magnitudes based on local Voronoi cell areas - smaller cells (indicating high constraint density) get smaller mutations, while larger cells get larger mutations. This should lead to more stable convergence and better exploration of the solution space.
</DESCRIPTION>
