<DESCRIPTION>
This change introduces Voronoi-based constraint density weighting to make the mutation operator more intelligent. Instead of uniform random mutations, we now calculate Voronoi cell areas for each circle and adjust mutation step sizes based on local constraint density. Circles in high-density regions (smaller Voronoi cells) get smaller mutations to preserve stability, while those in low-density regions can have larger mutations for exploration. This builds upon the existing evolutionary framework while making it more sophisticated and likely to converge to better solutions.
</DESCRIPTION>
