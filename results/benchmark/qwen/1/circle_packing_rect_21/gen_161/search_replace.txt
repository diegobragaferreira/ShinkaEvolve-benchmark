<DESCRIPTION>
I'll implement a more sophisticated Voronoi-based adaptive mutation strategy that weights radius adjustments based on local Voronoi cell areas. This approach will use the Voronoi diagram to identify constraint-dense regions and apply smaller mutation steps in those areas while allowing larger steps in sparse regions, which should lead to better convergence and higher radii sums.

The key insight is that in constraint-dense regions (small Voronoi cells), small radius adjustments can cause large constraint violations, so we should use smaller mutation steps. Conversely, in sparse regions (large Voronoi cells), larger adjustments are safer and more effective.
