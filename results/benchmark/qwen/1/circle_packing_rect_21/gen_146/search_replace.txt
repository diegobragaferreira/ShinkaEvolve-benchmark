This improvement implements Voronoi-based adaptive mutation with criticality weighting for radius adjustments. The key insight is to compute Voronoi diagrams for each configuration and weight radius mutations inversely to local Voronoi cell area, where smaller cells indicate higher constraint density requiring finer adjustments. This builds directly on the current program's successful Voronoi-based criticality assessment pattern by incorporating the progressive radius adjustment technique from successful generations, allowing the algorithm to apply smaller mutation steps (0.001) in highly constrained regions while permitting larger changes (up to 0.01) in sparse areas, thereby improving stability and convergence while respecting local geometry.
