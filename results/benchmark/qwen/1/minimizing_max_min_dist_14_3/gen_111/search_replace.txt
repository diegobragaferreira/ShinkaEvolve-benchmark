<DESCRIPTION>
The current implementation uses fixed constraints which may not be optimal for the optimization process. I'll implement adaptive constraint tightening that starts with looser bounds during initial exploration and progressively tightens constraints as optimization progresses. This approach helps with convergence while avoiding premature constraint violations.

The key insight is to gradually enforce stricter constraints (like maximum distance bounds) during optimization, allowing more flexible exploration in early stages and ensuring feasibility in later stages.
