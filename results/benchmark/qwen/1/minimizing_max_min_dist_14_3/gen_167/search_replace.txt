<DESCRIPTION>
The current program uses multiple initialization methods but could benefit from a more sophisticated approach that combines the best properties of different methods. The main issue is that the Sobol sequence initialization isn't being used effectively in the multi-start strategy. I'll enhance the initialization by creating a more diverse and effective set of starting points that combine Sobol sequences with perturbations, which should lead to better exploration of the solution space and potentially higher min/max ratios.

The key insight is to leverage the low-discrepancy properties of Sobol sequences while adding strategic perturbations to break symmetries and improve optimization outcomes.
