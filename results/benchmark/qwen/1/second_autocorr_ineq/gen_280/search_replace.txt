<DESCRIPTION>
The current enhanced initialization uses simple multi-scale alternating patterns and Gaussian peaks. I'll enhance this by incorporating spectral patterns that are mathematically known to produce favorable autoconvolution properties. These spectral patterns include sinusoidal components and frequency-domain considerations that naturally lead to flatter convolution profiles, which should improve the C₂ ratio.

The key insight is that autoconvolutions with flatter profiles (lower infinity norm relative to L1 norm) will yield higher C₂ values. Spectral patterns like sinusoids tend to create more structured and favorable convolution outcomes compared to purely random or simple alternating patterns.
