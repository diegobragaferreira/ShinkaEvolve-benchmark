1. Uses Voronoi cell areas to estimate appropriate radii (larger cells can accommodate larger circles)
2. Implements a more intelligent sampling strategy that prioritizes regions with good packing potential
3. Adds better handling of edge cases and improves robustness

This enhancement should provide better starting configurations that are closer to optimal, thus accelerating convergence of the overall optimization process.
