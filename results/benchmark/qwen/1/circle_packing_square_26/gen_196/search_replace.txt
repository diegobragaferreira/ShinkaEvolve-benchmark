The key insight is to enhance the Voronoi-based initialization by incorporating a more sophisticated approach for determining initial radii that better reflects the actual optimal packing geometry. Instead of using a simple Voronoi cell size estimation, we can implement a method that calculates approximate optimal radii based on the local density and geometric constraints in the Voronoi structure. This should provide better starting points for the evolutionary algorithm to converge faster toward higher-quality solutions.

The specific improvement involves replacing the current radius calculation in the Voronoi initialization with a more mathematically grounded approach that considers both the Voronoi cell area and the constraint geometry.
