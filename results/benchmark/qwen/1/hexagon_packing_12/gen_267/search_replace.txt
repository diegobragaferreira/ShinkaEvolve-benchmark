<DESCRIPTION>
Adding symmetry handling to exploit the 6-fold rotational symmetry of the optimal hexagon packing configuration. The idea is to reduce the search space by constraining the optimization to respect symmetry properties, particularly the fact that optimal solutions should exhibit approximate 6-fold symmetry. This approach will significantly reduce the dimensionality of the optimization problem and guide the search towards physically meaningful solutions.

The key insight is that in an optimal hexagon packing, we expect:
1. Central hexagon position should remain at origin
2. Ring structures should maintain rotational symmetry
3. The arrangement should be invariant under 60-degree rotations

I'll modify the optimization function to use a symmetry-aware approach that maintains these structural constraints during the optimization process.
