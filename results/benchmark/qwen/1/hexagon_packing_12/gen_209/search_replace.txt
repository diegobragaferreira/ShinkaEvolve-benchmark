<DESCRIPTION>
Adding numba JIT compilation to the core geometric computation functions will significantly improve performance by compiling the hot paths (like overlap checking and vertex generation) to machine code. This addresses the computational bottleneck in the hexagon packing problem where many geometric operations are repeated during optimization.

The key areas to target with numba:
1. Hexagon vertex generation
2. Bounding circle calculations
3. Fast overlap checking with early rejection
4. Spatial indexing operations

This follows the pattern from successful generations (151, 160, 161, etc.) that achieved 0.1 scores through Numba JIT compilation.
