<DESCRIPTION>
Implement Numba JIT compilation for the most computationally intensive functions to significantly speed up the hexagon packing optimization. The key functions to JIT compile are `create_unit_hexagon` (which creates polygons), `calculate_outer_hexagon_radius` (which processes all hexagon data), and `check_overlap_fast` (which performs overlap checks). This will eliminate Python overhead and enable much faster iteration cycles during optimization, which is crucial given the tight time constraint of 180 seconds.

def create_unit_hexagon(center=(0, 0), rotation=0):
    """Create a unit regular hexagon as a Shapely polygon"""
    angle_step = np.pi / 3
    points = []
