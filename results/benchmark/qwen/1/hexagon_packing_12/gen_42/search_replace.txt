<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement implements a local L-BFGS-B refinement step after the differential evolution optimization to fine-tune the hexagon positions and orientations. This hybrid approach leverages both global exploration (DE) and local exploitation (L-BFGS-B) to potentially achieve better solutions than DE alone. The key insight is that while DE is good at exploring the search space globally, L-BFGS-B can refine solutions locally to improve the objective function value. I'm adding this after the DE optimization but before returning the final result, using SciPy's minimize function with L-BFGS-B method.
</DESCRIPTION>
