use_adaptive_population_size
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
The idea is to implement adaptive population sizing in the differential evolution algorithm based on the optimization progress. This approach will help balance exploration vs exploitation more effectively - using larger populations in early stages for better exploration and smaller populations in later stages for faster convergence. This addresses the inefficiency in the current fixed population size approach.
</DESCRIPTION>
