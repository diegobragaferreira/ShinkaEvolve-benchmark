The current implementation uses a fixed number of iterations for local optimization methods, which may not be optimal for convergence. I'll implement adaptive early stopping criteria that monitors the objective value changes during optimization to detect when improvement plateaus, allowing us to terminate early and save computational resources while maintaining solution quality.

This approach will enhance the robustness of the L-BFGS-B and SLSQP optimization phases by dynamically determining when sufficient improvement has been achieved, rather than using fixed iteration limits that might be too restrictive or too permissive.
