Adding multi-start optimization with diverse structured initializations to enhance global exploration. This approach executes the entire DE-SLSQP-L-BFGS-B pipeline from 3-5 distinct structured initial configurations - such as a hexagonal grid, a golden spiral arrangement, and the existing modified grid pattern - then selecting the best result among all runs. This directly applies the proven multi-start strategy observed in previous successful generations, which significantly increased robustness and convergence reliability. By diversifying the starting points using multiple well-established geometric layouts, we can further reduce the risk of getting trapped in suboptimal solutions while maintaining the proven efficacy of the hybrid optimization framework.
