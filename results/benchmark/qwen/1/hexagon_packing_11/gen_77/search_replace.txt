Adding adaptive mutation rate scheduling to the differential evolution algorithm. The idea is to start with a high mutation rate (0.8) for early generations to encourage diverse exploration, then gradually decrease it to 0.1 in later generations to promote convergence toward optimal solutions. This helps balance global exploration and local exploitation, which should improve the packing ratio beyond the current benchmark of 0.2544.
