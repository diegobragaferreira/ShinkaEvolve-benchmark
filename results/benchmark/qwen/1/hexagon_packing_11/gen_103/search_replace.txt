Adding adaptive mutation rate scheduling to improve convergence behavior. The key change is to introduce a time-varying mutation rate that starts high (0.8) for early generations to encourage diverse exploration, then gradually decreases to 0.1 in later generations to promote convergence toward optimal solutions. This builds upon the proven effectiveness of adaptive control mechanisms shown in successful programs that avoided premature convergence.
