By using a spatial data structure like a grid or quadtree, we can significantly reduce the number of expensive intersection tests needed.

The key insight is that we don't need to check every pair of hexagons for overlap - we can use spatial partitioning to quickly identify which hexagons might actually collide based on their positions.
