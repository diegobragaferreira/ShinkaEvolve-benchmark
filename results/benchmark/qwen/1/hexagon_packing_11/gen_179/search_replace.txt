<DESCRIPTION>
Adding multi-start optimization with diverse initial configurations to escape local optima. This approach runs the optimization from multiple different starting points to explore various regions of the solution space and increase the chances of finding a better global optimum. The key insight is that hexagon packing problems often have many local optima, and a single starting point may get trapped in a suboptimal region.
